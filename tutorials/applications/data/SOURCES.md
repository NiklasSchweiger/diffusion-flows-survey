# Data of the §7 tutorial

All files here are derived from public-domain or CC0 sources. They are downscaled or reduced, never edited by hand.

## frames.jpg, frames.json
Frames of six photographic sequences by Eadweard Muybridge (1878 to 1887), public domain. Taken from the animated GIFs on Wikimedia Commons, converted to grey, centre-cropped to 1.6:1 and resized to 128 × 80 pixels (JPEG, quality 88). One row per clip, in this order:
1. File:Muybridge race horse animated.gif (15 frames)
2. File:Muybridge horse gallop animated 2.gif (12 frames)
3. File:Muybridge horse pacing animated.gif (20 frames)
4. File:Muybridge horse walking animated.gif (9 frames)
5. File:Muybridge Buffalo galloping.gif (16 frames)
6. File:Muybridge horse jumping animated.gif (first 32 frames)

## structures.json
- proteins: backbone atoms N, Cα, C of chain A (first model, first alternate location) and the secondary structure from the HELIX and SHEET records of RCSB PDB entries 1UBQ (ubiquitin), 1ENH (engrailed homeodomain) and 1L2Y (Trp-cage, NMR model 1). PDB data are CC0.
- molecules: the 3D conformers (with hydrogens) and bond tables of PubChem CIDs 2519 (caffeine), 2244 (aspirin), 3672 (ibuprofen), 1983 (paracetamol) and 89594 (nicotine). PubChem data are public domain.
